Breakfast Dinner Dessert Bread Soup

Breads

White Bread

Ingredients

  • 3 c. Flour
  • 1.5 t. Yeast
  • 1 T. Sugar
  • 1 T. Oil
  • 1.5 T. Salt
  • 1.5 c. Water
  • Equipment: kettle, large mixing bowl, large mixing spoon (wooden), 4 cup glass measuring cup, plastic wrap, dish

  1. 1. Activate the Yeast: Mix the warm water, sugar, and yeast in a large bowl. Let it sit for 5 to 10 minutes until it becomes frothy.
  2. 2. Mix the Dough: Add the salt, oil, and flour into the yeast mixture. Stir with a spoon or spatula until a shaggy dough forms.
  3. 3. Knead the Dough: Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface. Knead with your hands for about 5 minutes until it becomes smooth, soft, and elastic.
  4. 4. First Rise: Place the dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover it with a clean towel or plastic wrap, and let it rise in a warm spot for about 1 to 1.5 hours until it doubles in size.
  5. 5. Shape the Loaf: Punch down the dough to release the air. Shape it into a neat loaf and place it into a greased 8x4-inch loaf pan.
  6. 6. Second Rise: Cover the pan and let the dough rise again for 40 to 60 minutes until it looks puffy and fills the pan.
  7. 7. Bake: Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Bake the bread for 30 to 35 minutes until the crust is golden brown and sounds hollow when tapped on the bottom.
  8. 8. Cool: Remove the bread from the pan immediately and let it cool completely on a wire rack for at least 30 minutes before slicing.
